Case Description

The plaintiff, Sterling Equities, alleges a fundamental breach of the Master Service Agreement (MSA) dated Oct 12, 2023. Global Logistics Int'l failed to deliver critical supply chain infrastructure within the specified 90-day window, resulting in estimated damages exceeding $2.4M. The defense maintains that force majeure clauses apply due to regional port closures.
